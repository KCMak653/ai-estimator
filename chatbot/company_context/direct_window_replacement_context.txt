# About us

## Our story

Direct Windows was founded in 2003 in Toronto by first generation Canadians. The founder, Sergey, started his journey in the window business as a window installer. With hundreds of window installations under his belt, he decided to branch out and start his own shop.

He recognized a need in the market for high quality windows that don’t break the bank. By following a simple motto – “what kind of windows would I want to install in my own home?”, Sergey was able to grow Direct Windows into what it is today.

Through his experience as an installer, Sergey hand-picks his installation teams to make sure they are able to complete every job to the highest standards of quality. Getting it right the first time means we don’t have to return for service calls which saves you time and reduces our overall operational costs

These days, Direct Windows and the team are focused on growth through learning and experience. By tackling more complex jobs and challenging ourselves to exceed your expectations, we are producing more qualified installation teams and satisfied customers that promote our brand through referrals and recommendations.

At Direct Windows, we strongly believe that we can get what we want by helping you, the customer, get what you want. That is why we are proudly committed to serving you and your needs. Give us a call today and let us show you how our values can help you and your family.

## Window Replacement Services

Direct windows is a supplier and installer of residential and commercial windows. We source top quality products and offer a warranty for all products and services we provide. We are pleased to offer a variety of windows to fit all your functional needs and aesthetic desires.


#### Window Styles

#### We offer windows in a variety of styles and designs. With available add-on features, grill designs and shapes, we are able to produce any window to suit your needs. We can also help you match a window colour to your existing interior or exterior decor.

Depending on your specific requirements, our window styles can fulfill various needs. An outward-opening awning window will usually work best over a kitchen sink to allow for fresh air and the maximizing of interior space. Outswing casement windows offer great insulation. Slider windows are perfect for when you need a window for a large opening that is easy to operate.


#### Energy Efficiency

Our windows are energy star certified and are suitable for all climate zones in southern Ontario. All windows installed by Direct Windows feature standard double pane glass and argon gas.


#### Installation

All of our installers are fully certified and insured. Every project is assigned a supervisor that is responsible for making sure that the work is completed to the highest standard and your property is spotless.

We pride ourselves in the ability to work fast and efficiently. Our crew is able to complete most jobs in a single day because we make sure that we have enough workers to get the job fast. The sooner we finish the work, the less we disrupt your home and daily life.

Together with a high quality window, we consider installation to be the heart of our business. A proper installation can make all the difference between a window that will last you a lifetime and one that will cause leaks and damage to the foundation of your home. Our crews have been working with us for a decade and have a stake in our business. This is the only way to ensure that they feel personal responsibility for completing your work with the highest possible standards.
